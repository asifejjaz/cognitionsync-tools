# Filework Portfolio Roadmap

The owner's long-term intention is to build the complete 120-candidate portfolio, test demand, and invest more in tools that perform. The complete researched list and source links are preserved in [docs/portfolio-research.md](docs/portfolio-research.md). Treat this as project memory for future sessions, not as a commitment that every candidate is equally viable.

## Release 0.1: Authorized Scope

1. Image compression to a requested KB limit.
2. Image resize and crop.
3. HEIC/HEIF to JPG or PNG.
4. WebP conversion in both directions.
5. Photo and signature upload preparation.
6. JPG/PNG to PDF.
7. Merge PDFs.
8. Split PDF ranges, individual pages, or extract into one PDF.
9. Rotate, reorder, and remove PDF pages.
10. Visible signature and text on PDF, not certificate signing.
11. DOCX to Markdown, with embedded image bundles.
12. Markdown to selectable-text PDF.

## Later Releases

Prioritize the remaining researched candidates using measured demand, cost per successful task, repeat usage, conversion accuracy, organic search impressions, and sustainable operating cost. Paid campaign performance and organic rankings must be tracked separately. Paid traffic does not establish organic search ranking.

PDF compression, DOCX to PDF, OCR, QR generation, data conversion, and specialist calculators are candidates, not part of this release. Downloader products require a separate copyright, platform-terms, and ad-policy review before implementation.

## Before Public Launch

- Choose the permanent brand/domain and supply VM deployment access securely.
- Review privacy text, contact information, sample licensing, and accessibility.
- Configure TLS, CDN caching, monitoring, and the actual SITE_URL.
- Add consent-aware analytics and successful-task events without sending filenames, document contents, or signatures.
- Apply for advertising approval and review ad placement policies. No ads or trackers are included in this release.
- Measure on real phones and expand format/fidelity tests before removing release limits.

Browser-side processing reduces VM conversion load; it does not establish tested high-traffic capacity or eliminate ongoing maintenance.
